## Initial Post

Tell your classmates something interesting or concerning about the *datasets* that generative AI models were trained on. Your post should be:

- **Substantive**: you'll probably need 100-200 words.
- **Factual**: give a specific source or example.
- **Specific**: name specific models or datasets; give specific examples.
- **Unique**: try to pick something that hasn't been mentioned yet.
- **Clear**: write in a way that is easy to understand.

The best posts should also be **provocative** or **controversial**, but back up your claims with evidence.
