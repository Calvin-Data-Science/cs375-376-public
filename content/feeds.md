revised: 2025

AI technology is changing rapidly. It's helpful to subscribe to a few curated feeds of content.

I suggest you pick one or two *types* of updates that you normally process, and then pick one or two instances of each type.

Good curators are hard to find and keep changing. I'll suggest a few below, but **please share what you find** (and if any of my recommendations have gotten stale).


## Blogs and Email Newsletters

I like subscribing by email or on [NetNewsWire](https://netnewswire.com/) (an RSS reader) so I don't have to "poll" for updates. Again, you should search on your own:

- **Critical perspectives**
    - [AI: A Guide for Thinking Humans | Melanie Mitchell | Substack](https://aiguide.substack.com/) *critical but not bashing*
    - [Mystery AI Hype Theater 3000: The Newsletter](https://buttondown.com/maiht3k) ([archives](https://buttondown.com/maiht3k/archive/))
    - [Gary Marcus on AI](https://garymarcus.substack.com/) *critical perspective*
    - [Civics of Technology blog](https://www.civicsoftechnology.org/blog/) *policy and societal implications*
- **Technical perspectives**
    - [Simon Willison](https://simonwillison.net/) *AI applications and perspectives*
    - [Ahead of AI | Sebastian Raschka, PhD | Substack](https://magazine.sebastianraschka.com/)
    - [Interconnects | Nathan Lambert](https://www.interconnects.ai/) *technically deep*

Other good ones to explore:

- [AlgorithmWatch](https://algorithmwatch.org/en/) *general tech ethics and policy*
- [🤗 Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/blog) *lots of hands-on updates*
